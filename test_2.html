DYMO Local Service Test

This page checks whether the DYMO local web service is reachable (often on https://127.0.0.1:41951). It also helps you tell the difference between: service not running, TLS/cert issues, and CORS blocking.
Tip: also try https://localhost:41951 if 127.0.0.1 fails, or vice versa.
